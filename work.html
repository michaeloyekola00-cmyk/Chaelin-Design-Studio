Work

What we'd fix, and why.

Ad campaigns we've made for local trades, and concept redesigns that show how we'd fix real websites.

The website redesigns below are concepts, made to show our approach. They are not client projects, and no results are claimed. Our ad campaigns are further down.
Concept

Drywall & restoration contractor

Idaho, USA

What we found
  • The site's title was just "." in Google and browser tabs
  • No services or call button on the homepage
  • Every service showed a "$0.00" price
  • Before-and-after photos hidden on the gallery page
What we changed
  • Their own line, "From Damage to Done", as a bold headline
  • Call and free-quote buttons up top
  • Three clear paths: homeowners, mitigation partners, investors
  • Real before-and-afters and readable reviews on the homepage
  • A quote form that asks for the job type
Concept

Apostille, fingerprinting & notary service

Alabama, USA

What we found
  • 103 five-star Google reviews that showed as an empty block on phones
  • A stock photo instead of the founder
  • Separate booking forms for each service
  • The founder's 15 years in healthcare buried in the bio
What we changed
  • 5.0 rating and 103 reviews visible on every screen
  • A simple four-step apostille process
  • "Precise. Confidential. Bonded." as a clear promise
  • One booking form where people tap the service they need
  • A seal-style logo concept
Concept

Property management company

Wyoming, USA

What we found
  • The site didn't fit on a phone: menu and boxes ran off the screen
  • A lightning storm as the first impression
  • Owner services and free consultation buried on inner pages
  • No team, no reviews, © 2019 in the footer
What we changed
  • Their own tagline as the headline
  • Two clear paths: property owners and renters
  • Owner services grouped into three simple blocks
  • Tenant tools (rent, listings, portals) kept one tap away
  • Space for the team and reviews
Concept

Interior decorator & color consultant

New York, USA

What we found
  • The headline sat in a faded box over a busy photo
  • Captions written for Google, shown to visitors
  • Few photos, no client quotes on the homepage
  • Best of Houzz badge tiny in the footer
What we changed
  • Her work as the hero, with a clear headline beside it
  • Four services as visual cards with "Ideal for" lines
  • Awards moved to where people decide
  • A calm discovery-call form
