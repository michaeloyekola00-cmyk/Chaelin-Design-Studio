How we work

A clear process. No surprises.

Here's exactly what happens, how long it takes and what we need from you, from the free audit to your first monthly report.

Step 01 · 48 hours

Free Lead Leak Score

We look at your business the way a customer does: searching on Google, landing on your website, deciding whether to call.

What we do
  • Your website on desktop and mobile
  • Your Google Business Profile and reviews
  • How easy it is to call or request a quote
  • What your top local competitors do better
What we need from you

Your website address and your business name. That's it.

What you get

Your score out of 100 and a short, plain-English report (PDF or video) with your biggest leaks and what to fix first. Yours to keep, even if we never work together.

Step 02 · 30 minutes

Plan call

We talk through your score, your services, your area and the kind of jobs you want more of.

What we do
  • Your best services and most profitable jobs
  • The area you cover
  • What you've tried before
  • Your budget and timeline
What we need from you

30 minutes on a call. Book a time

What you get

A clear plan and a fixed quote. No pressure: you decide if and when to go ahead.

Step 03 · 2–3 weeks

Build

We design and build your website, write the words, and get your Google presence ready.

What we do
  • Homepage design for your approval first
  • Copy written from your services and reviews
  • The full site, mobile-first
  • Call and form tracking, analytics
  • Google Business Profile updates
What we need from you

Your logo, photos of your work (phone photos are fine), and one round of feedback.

What you get

A finished website for review, with up to two rounds of changes.

Step 04 · Launch week

Launch

We put everything live and switch the system on.

What we do
  • Site live on your domain
  • Review requests switched on
  • Missed-call text back and lead alerts set up
  • A quick walk-through, so you know how everything works
What we need from you

Access to your domain and Google Business Profile (we'll show you how).

What you get

A live site, a tuned Google profile and a working lead system.

Step 05 · Every month

Grow

On a monthly plan, we keep improving what brings in leads.

What we do
  • Track calls, forms and reviews
  • Improve pages and your Google profile
  • Run and adjust ads (Leads + Ads and Growth plans)
  • Test small changes that lift enquiries
What we need from you

Replying to the leads that come in.

What you get

A plain-English report every month: how many leads, where they came from, and what we're doing next. Plus the 90-day promise: more leads than your baseline, or your monthly fees back.

The monthly cycle

Track. Report. Improve.

Real numbers every month, and a 90-day promise: more leads, or your money back.

Track

Every call, form and review is counted and tagged by where it came from.

Report

Improve
