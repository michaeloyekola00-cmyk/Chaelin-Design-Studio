The 90-day promise

More leads in 90 days, or your money back.

We're confident in the system, so we take the risk, not you. Here's exactly how it works, in plain English.

1
We measure your starting pointBefore we begin, we agree your baseline together: your calls and form enquiries from the 90 days before, using your phone records, Google Business Profile and website data.
2
We track every leadFrom launch, every call and form enquiry is tracked and shown in your monthly report.
3
If leads don't go up, you get a refundIf your tracked leads in the first 90 days after launch aren't higher than your baseline, we refund the monthly fees you paid for those 90 days.

What we ask from youStay on the plan for the 90 days, reply to the leads that come in, and give us the access and photos we need during setup.

Applies to every plan. Covers the monthly fees you paid, not ad spend paid to Google or Meta. Refunds are paid within 14 days of the 90-day report.

Questions

About plans.

Why "from" prices?

Every business is different: a one-van plumber and a 20-person contractor need different things. The prices above are where most plans start. After your free Lead Leak Score and a short call, you get a fixed quote before anything starts.

Do you build websites on their own?

No. A website on its own rarely brings in more leads, so we don't sell it separately. Every plan includes a lead-ready website, built and improved as part of the system.

Is there a setup or build fee?

No. Your website and setup are included in the monthly plan. The minimum term is 3 months, which is the same 90 days our promise covers.

What does the 90-day promise cover?

The monthly fees you pay on any plan during the first 90 days after launch. It doesn't cover ad spend paid to Google or Meta. Full terms are above.

What do you need from me?

A plan call, photos of your work and one round of feedback during the build. After launch, just reply to your leads.
