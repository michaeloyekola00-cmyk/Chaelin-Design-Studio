Privacy Policy

We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to reply to you and do the work you ask for.

What we collect

When you fill in a form, we collect your name, business name, website, email address, phone number (if given) and your message.

How we use it

To send your free Lead Leak Score, reply to your enquiry, and deliver services you agree to. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list without your permission.

Who processes it

Form submissions are handled by our form provider (Formspree) and delivered to our email. Our website is hosted on Vercel. These providers process data only to provide their services.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 2 years, or for as long as needed to provide services and meet legal obligations.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email chaelin@chaelinstudios.com and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.
