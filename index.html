The problem

Most websites leak leads.

In the local business websites we review, the same three problems come up again and again. None of them are about looks.

01 · FOUND

People can't find you

A thin Google profile, a missing page title, no local pages. Customers search, and your competitor shows up first.

02 · TRUST

People don't trust you yet

Reviews hidden on another page, stock photos, no face behind the business. Visitors leave to "think about it".

03 · ACTION

People can't reach you easily

No call button on mobile, a layout that breaks on phones, a form nobody answers. Ready customers slip away.
